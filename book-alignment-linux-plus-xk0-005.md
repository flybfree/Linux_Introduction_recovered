# Companion Reading Map — CompTIA Linux+ All-in-One Exam Guide (XK0-005)

## Book identification

Ted Jordan and Sandor Strohmayer, *CompTIA Linux+ Certification All-in-One Exam Guide, Second Edition (Exam XK0-005)*, McGraw Hill, 2023. The print edition ISBN-13 is **9781264798964** (ISBN-10 **1264798962**). The ebook uses a different ISBN.

This map uses the book's published chapter titles and contents. The course and book are organized differently: our 14 lessons teach introductory concepts gradually, while the book is a broader certification guide with advanced administration and exam practice. Read the course lesson first, then use the mapped book sections to reinforce it. “Selected” means read the relevant subsection and defer procedures or exercises that require topics not yet taught or a system-wide change.

## Book chapters with no single course-lesson equivalent

All 20 chapters connect to at least one lesson above, but some book chapters contain material spread across the course or beyond its current scope:

- **Chapter 17 — Applying DevOps: Automation and Orchestration** connects to Lesson 14's project documentation and optional Git use. The course does not teach full DevOps workflows or orchestration.
- **Chapter 18 — Understanding Virtualization and the Cloud** supports Lesson 1's lab-environment context and can extend the capstone. Containers, Kubernetes, cloud storage, and provisioning are beyond the 14-lesson core.
- **Chapter 20 — Installing and Configuring Linux** supplements Lesson 1's installation and setup discussion. Its broader desktop configuration topics are not all included in the introductory course.

## Suggested student workflow

1. Complete the assigned course lesson and lab first.
2. Read the corresponding chapter sections in the table; use the chapter review questions for independent practice if desired.
3. Mark unfamiliar or more advanced material for later rather than assuming it is required for the course assessment.
4. Compare commands and procedures with the instructor's current lab directions. The book is broader than the course, and some exercises make system changes that are outside our classroom safety boundaries.
5. Use the book's chapter review and exam resources as extra practice, not as a replacement for the course's labs and Moodle banks.
