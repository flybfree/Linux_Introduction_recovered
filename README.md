# Introduction to Linux Curriculum

This repository contains the materials for a 14-lesson introductory Linux course.

## Project structure

- `lesson-01-prototype.md` — Lesson 1 textbook chapter and supporting lesson materials
- `lesson-01-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Moodle XML question bank for Lesson 1
- `lesson-02-linux-shell.md` — Lesson 2 textbook chapter, lab, assessment, glossary, and instructor materials
- `lesson-02-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Moodle XML question bank for Lesson 2
- `lesson-03-files-directories.md` and `lesson-03-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 3 materials
- `lesson-04-users-groups-permissions.md` and `lesson-04-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 4 materials
- `lesson-05-processes-job-control.md` and `lesson-05-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 5 materials
- `lesson-06-software-package-management.md` and `lesson-06-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 6 materials
- `lesson-07-shell-scripting-automation.md` and `lesson-07-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 7 materials
- `lesson-08-text-processing-pipelines.md` and `lesson-08-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 8 materials
- `lesson-09-storage-filesystems-backups.md` and `lesson-09-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 9 materials
- `lesson-10-networking-fundamentals.md` and `lesson-10-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 10 materials
- `lesson-11-services-system-administration.md` and `lesson-11-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 11 materials
- `lesson-11-demo.service` — harmless user-level service unit used in the Lesson 11 lab
- `lesson-12-security-safe-administration.md` and `lesson-12-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 12 materials
- `lesson-13-troubleshooting-documentation.md` and `lesson-13-moodle-question-bank.xml` — Lesson 13 materials
- `course-progression.md` — Roadmap for all 14 lessons

Each lesson will be developed as an independent module while maintaining a coherent progression across the course. A completed lesson is expected to include textbook content, hands-on activities, assessments, and instructor-support materials.

## Development standards

- Ubuntu Desktop LTS is the default student environment unless a lesson specifies otherwise.
- Each chapter includes a glossary as part of the textbook chapter.
- Each chapter includes at least 20 Moodle XML questions.
- Question banks include both conceptual questions and questions about Linux commands introduced in the chapter.
- Changes should be reviewed for technical accuracy, instructional progression, command safety, and consistency with earlier lessons.
